# Concept

## The Story

**Relentless Raven** — our AI hip-hop artist character — runs a cypher at the VeeDome. He's teaching Hip Hop 101 to two students:

- **Dynamic Dinosaur** — the decisive, action-first VeeFriend. His lesson: commit to the beat, don't overthink it.
- **Perfect Persian Cat** — the trendsetter who expects perfection. Her lesson: polish matters, but perfection is a process, not a starting point.

The three trade bars, then close the cypher together. It's a rap lesson disguised as a story — which is exactly how VeeFriends teaches traits.

## Goals

1. **Get Gary Vee's attention** through proof of work, not a pitch.
2. **Demonstrate the AI audio lab's capabilities** — multi-character voice separation, structured lyric generation, reproducible workflow.
3. **Show scientific, documented process** — every prompt, every generation, every decision logged.

## Characters & Voice Design

| Character | Voice profile | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Relentless Raven | Deep, gravelly male baritone | Confident, teacher energy, slight rasp |
| Dynamic Dino | Bright, energetic male | Fast, punchy, enthusiastic |
| Perfect Persian Cat | Smooth, cool female alto | Laid-back, precise, a little smug |
