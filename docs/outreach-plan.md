# Outreach Plan

## Principle

Gary Vee's documented advice: don't ask for attention — drop proof of work. His own words (paraphrased from his public posts): "Do the work upfront. Drop the link in their DM with a 2-line message. Zero entitlement, pure proof of work. They will notice."

## Sequence

1. **Finish the track** — multi-pass assembly, final mix, exported audio.
2. **Post organically first** — per Gary's own rule: every piece of content made for a sale or outreach gets posted organically before it's pitched. Tag @veefriends and @garyvee. Use the hashtag #VeeFriends.
3. **DM Gary Vee** — two lines max. Something like: "Made a fan track — Relentless Raven teaching Hip Hop 101 to Dynamic Dino and Perfect Persian Cat. Full process is open source: [repo link]. No ask, just sharing."
4. **Engage the community** — reply to VeeFriends fan posts, share in relevant Discords and X communities. Depth and volume, not one shot.

## What NOT to Do

- Don't pitch services in the first message.
- Don't use official VeeFriends artwork.
- Don't claim commercial rights to the characters.
- Don't spam — one thoughtful drop beats ten desperate ones.

## The Repo as the Resume

This GitHub repo IS the pitch. It shows: documented process, reproducible methodology, scientific logging, and a real deliverable. If Gary Vee or his team clicks through, they see a professional operation, not a one-off meme.
